Product Listing - QA Testing & Bug Fixing

Implementation
- Created a new Angular 21 application using standalone components.
- Added the required product model, product service, cart service, and product-list component.
- Preserved the assignment product data:
  Laptop - $999 - Electronics - Stock: 5
  Phone - $699 - Electronics - Stock: 0
  Desk - $299 - Furniture - Stock: 10
  Chair - $199 - Furniture - Stock: 3
- Added locally stored SVG illustrations so product images do not depend on external image hosts.
- Added a responsive product grid, category dropdown, filtered product count, stock labels, disabled out-of-stock action, cart badge, and accessible success notification.

Four assignment bugs addressed
1. Cart count not showing: CartService initializes its cart stream to an empty cart and emits the count (initially 0) as cart contents change.
2. Category filter not working: ProductService filters its existing product data by the selected category; All Products, Electronics, and Furniture show the correct products.
3. No feedback when adding to cart: Successful additions show a live success notification. CartService rejects products with no stock, and the Phone button is disabled.
4. Product count not updating: The displayed count derives from the filtered products observable and updates automatically when the category changes.
